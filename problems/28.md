# Problem 28 · A base-change bound for the descent solve

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 4 ([Attention as descent](../THESES.md#thesis-4)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], Table 13.16.13, entry O14, pp. 3174–3175. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** open.

**Statement.** Prove “a base-change bound for the descent solve”: a bound relating the outputs of the descent solve of [S02, Ch. 13.10] at two bases, on which every cross-base stability statement of the book depends.

**What the source establishes.** The register records where the argument stops: “no bound relates the solve at two bases; the available Jacobian square measures a different quantity”.

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** Spectral theory of sheaf Laplacians [HG19]; perturbation bounds [Hig02].

**Solved when.** A proved bound, with explicit constants, on the difference of the descent solves at two bases in terms of a stated distance between the bases, or a family of examples showing that no such bound holds.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Work on sheaf diffusion and its stability was found; no base-change bound for this solve was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 28](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/28). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
