# Problem 22 · Topos-MoE against modern sparse mixture-of-experts

**Kind.** Machine problem, research level. Thesis 4 ([Attention as descent](../THESES.md#thesis-4)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], §10.1.7.2, p. 1854; instrument N7, §9.1.9, pp. 1768–1769. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** D.

**Statement.** In Topos-MoE, the mixture-of-experts layer of [S02], each expert is an open sub-topos and routing constructs a sufficient cover. Run the registered comparison N7 of [S02, §9.1.9] against a modern fine-grained sparse mixture-of-experts at matched total and active parameters, executed FLOPs, wall time and communication. “Coverage routing must beat top-k on quality and on total cost; lowering its own deficiency score proves nothing about either.”

**What the source establishes.** The descent typing is proved; the benefit is not. In the released configuration the coverage router cannot yet be trained, and it “may not report the coverage router as a trained component until that backward exists and is finite-difference checked.” The thresholds are fixed in advance: “Confirmation requires a 10 per cent relative reduction in multi-expert composition error or a 3-point absolute improvement, while remaining within 1 per cent of the top-k baseline on single-expert tasks and within 10 per cent of its throughput.”

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** [SMM+17]; [FZS22]; categorical approaches to architectures [GLD+24; Mah25].

**Solved when.** A trainable coverage router with a finite-difference-checked backward pass, and the N7 comparison executed under the registered protocol, reporting confirmation or one of the registered null outcomes.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Many routing variants for mixture-of-experts were found; none uses sheaf-theoretic coverage routing with these controls.

**Discussion.** [Issue 22](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/22). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
