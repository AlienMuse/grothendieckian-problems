# Problem 8 · The AURORA-K0 kernel, II: a model and relative consistency

**Kind.** Formalization problem, research level. Thesis 2 ([Status is part of the type](../THESES.md#thesis-2)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. III, §70.19, pp. 9428–9429 (obligations K0-META-010 to K0-META-012). DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** O.

**Statement.** For the kernel K0 of Problem 7, discharge the semantic obligations of the registry: “At least one nontrivial model validates the rules”; “Relative consistency, if pursued”, relative to a named base theory; and “Conservativity over any source theory” for the source theories that the architecture embeds.

**What the source establishes.** The registry requires an explicit model construction with independent validation, a proposition-exact metatheorem for relative consistency, and an explicit interpretation with a conservativity proof, and records all three as open.

**Proposed by.** [S01].

**Background.** Models of type theory; [Uni13]; conservativity results for extensions of type theory.

**Solved when.** A machine-checked model of K0 that is not trivial in the sense of §70.19, and machine-checked proofs of relative consistency and of conservativity over at least one named source theory.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Mechanised relative-consistency proofs exist for other systems; none for K0 was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 8](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/8). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
