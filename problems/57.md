# Problem 57 · Coherence for arithmetic modes

**Kind.** Formalization problem, research level. Thesis 11 ([Creating language](../THESES.md#thesis-11)).

**Source.** *The Arithmetic Topos* [S07], Problem 6.29, p. 228. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23629681](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23629681).

**Status in the source.** CONJECTURE.

**Statement.** For the modal directed type theory $\mathrm{TT}^{\mathrm{comp}}_{\mathrm{KH}}$ of [S07, Ch. 6], construct a finite presentation of its mode two-category and “prove a coherence theorem deciding equality of generated two-cells”, while retaining the Beck–Chevalley, projection-formula, place-commutation, Frobenius and $\flat\dashv\sharp$ structures of the chapter.

**What the source establishes.** “A positive solution would not construct the semantic functors, but it would make their formal composites manageable. A negative solution would force a proof-relevant representation of coherence cells in the kernel and would weaken the decidability prospect.”

**Proposed by.** [S07].

**Background.** [GKNB21]; [Gra22]; [RS17].

**Solved when.** A finite presentation and a coherence theorem with a decision procedure for equality of generated two-cells, mechanised or proved on paper with all cases, or a proof that equality is undecidable for every finite presentation retaining the listed structures.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Multimodal type theory [GKNB21] and its normalization theorem, which reduces conversion to the conversion problem of the mode theory and gives decidable type checking for a decidable mode theory [Gra22], were found; no coherence theorem for these modes was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 57](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/57). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
