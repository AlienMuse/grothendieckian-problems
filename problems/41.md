# Problem 41 · The arithmetic flow

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, programme level. Thesis 8 ([The rising sea](../THESES.md#thesis-8)).

**Source.** *Fractured Adelic Geometry* [S04], Problem 53.2.4 (Obstruction IV), p. 416; Problem 4.5.1, p. 37. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23585887](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23585887).

**Status in the source.** OPEN.

**Statement.** Construct a strongly continuous flow on a cohomology theory over $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z$ whose primitive closed orbits are the primes, the orbit at $p$ having length $\log p$, with no spurious orbits and with a generator satisfying the weight adjunction. In the form of Problem 4.5.1: a foliated or laminated space with a flow and a leafwise cohomology admitting duality and regularized determinants, whose Lefschetz trace formula is the explicit formula.

**What the source establishes.** “The flow Φ𝑡 is postulated, not built. No ℝ≥0-action has ever been constructed on any cohomology theory over Specℤ whose primitive closed orbits are the primes with lengths log p”. For Problem 4.5.1: “No object satisfying these requirements is presently known.” The cyclotomic structure of THH supplies $\psi_p$ at each prime; the interpolation to a continuous action is missing.

**Proposed by.** Deninger [Den98]; restated, with the source’s axioms, in [S04].

**Background.** [Den98]; [NS18].

**Solved when.** A construction with the stated properties, or a no-go theorem showing that no $\mathbb R_{\ge0}$-action of the required kind exists on any cohomology of $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z$ (item (ii) of the refutation list of [S04, §2.8.1]).

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Trace formulas for foliated flows and regularized-determinant formulas in Deninger’s framework were found; no construction over Spec ℤ was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 41](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/41). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
