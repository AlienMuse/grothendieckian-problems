# Problem 33 · Formalizing uniform circuit classes

**Kind.** Formalization problem, research level. Thesis 9 ([State what is not established](../THESES.md#thesis-9)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], Open Problem 13.15.B, p. 3135; cut ledger, Table 13.15.7, p. 3133. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** OPEN.

**Statement.** “Formalise L-uniform circuit families and the AC$^0$ many-one reduction convention of Definition 2.14.5 to the point where clauses 4 and 5 of Theorem 13.13.24 become statable, and decide whether the Barrington construction of Chapter 2.14 is within reach of a proof term.”

**What the source establishes.** The source’s separation results use L-uniform circuit classes and Barrington’s theorem on paper; the machine-checked part stops at statements, and the cut ledger records that “a Barrington formalisation is a project in itself”.

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** [Bar89]; [BIS90]; a recent Lean formalisation of the non-uniform PARITY lower bound [Wes26].

**Solved when.** A Lean (or other assistant) library defining L-uniform circuit families and AC$^0$ many-one reductions in which clauses 4 and 5 of Theorem 13.13.24 are stated, together with either a formal proof of Barrington’s theorem or a documented estimate of what such a proof requires.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** The non-uniform PARITY lower bound has been formalised in Lean [Wes26]; uniformity and Barrington’s theorem were not found in mathlib or in formal-conjectures (GitHub code search).

**Discussion.** [Issue 33](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/33). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
