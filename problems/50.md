# Problem 50 · Toward an algorithm for prismatic cohomology

**Kind.** Machine problem, research level. Thesis 12 ([Judge a method by its open ledger](../THESES.md#thesis-12)).

**Source.** *Strained Prismatic Geometry* [S06], Obstruction 49.6.6, pp. 1029–1030. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23610034](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23610034).

**Status in the source.** OPEN.

**Statement.** “There is presently no general terminating algorithm which, from equations for an arbitrary p-adic formal scheme, computes its absolute prismatic cohomology together with derived completion, Nygaard filtration, Frobenius, and effective descent as an F-gauge”. “The frontier relevant to this book has five layers”: absolute descent; integral semistable gauges for curves with bad reduction, including the primes 2 and 3; the Habiro–prismatic comparison; archimedean assembly; and the global strain trace. Give a terminating, certified algorithm for one layer.

**What the source establishes.** The companion package of [S06] supplies finite inputs and hard failure states, and “implements none of these by renaming a local matrix.”

**Proposed by.** [S06].

**Background.** [BS22]; [BL22].

**Solved when.** A terminating algorithm for one of the five layers on a stated class of inputs, with a proof of correctness and an implementation whose outputs agree with known computations.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Explicit computations of prismatic and syntomic cohomology in special cases were found; no general algorithm for any of the five layers was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 50](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/50). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
