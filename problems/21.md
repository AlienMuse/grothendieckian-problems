# Problem 21 · Relative obstruction as a predictor of unsupported answers

**Kind.** Machine problem, research level. Thesis 5 ([Report failure; abstain without evidence](../THESES.md#thesis-5)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], §10.1.7.3, pp. 1854–1855; instrument N5, §9.1.7, p. 1766. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** T for the mathematics; D for what remains; N5 withdrawn.

**Statement.** For an evidence subdomain $A\subseteq X$, a coefficient sheaf $F$ and a partial section $s_A$, the connecting class $\partial(s_A)\in H^1(X,A;F)$ is a genuine obstruction to extending $s_A$. Supply the typed record that the source requires before this class may be used as an instrument, and test prospectively whether it predicts unsupported answers of a language model.

**What the source establishes.** The mathematics is proved; the instrument is not: “N5 is therefore WITHDRAWN as a validation or falsification instrument.” Reinstatement requires six items, including “at least one worked non-zero example, together with a zero control, computed before labels are revealed”. The remaining task is stated as “What remains is the difficult empirical part: the evidence sheaf must reflect factual support, the relative class must be approximated at scale, and prospective prediction must beat entropy, retrieval sufficiency, self-consistency and correctness probes without site collapse.”

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** [HG19]; [FKKG24]; [HRH24].

**Solved when.** The six reinstatement items of §9.1.7 published for one benchmark, followed by a prospective comparison against entropy, sequence likelihood, verbal confidence, retrieval sufficiency, self-consistency and a trained correctness probe, reported in either direction.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Hallucination detectors based on entropy and probes exist [FKKG24]; sheaf-based consistency rating has been proposed [HRH24]; no instrument based on a relative connecting class was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 21](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/21). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
