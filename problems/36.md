# Problem 36 · Polarization of a cyclotomic spectrum

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 8 ([The rising sea](../THESES.md#thesis-8)).

**Source.** *Arithmetic Spectral Descent* [S03], Problem 24.18, p. 539 (restated as Problems 29.8 and 30.16); Criterion 57.5, p. 1145. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23564898](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23564898).

**Status in the source.** O (open).

**Statement.** Define a polarization of a cyclotomic spectrum: extra data on Real topological Hochschild homology (THR) $(\mathrm{THR}(R),O(2),\{\varphi^{\mathbb R}_p\})$, generalising an ample class or a Kähler form, such that (i) it induces a real quadratic form on $\pi_*(\mathrm{THR}(R)^{hO(2)})$ through a trace state; (ii) “the form is definite on primitive parts with respect to a Lefschetz decomposition”; and (iii) for $X$ smooth projective over $\mathbb F_q$ with an ample line bundle, “it recovers the Hodge index theorem on crystalline cohomology / the positivity underlying the Weil conjectures.”

**What the source establishes.** The source explains why (iii) is already delicate: crystalline cohomology carries no real structure, and the Katz–Messing comparison transports eigenvalues but not the positivity mechanism. Criterion 57.5 fixes a test: a proposed polarization solves the problem only if, applied to the topological Hochschild homology $\mathrm{THH}(C)$ of a curve $C$ over $\mathbb F_q$ of genus $g\ge1$, it proves $|\alpha_i|=\sqrt q$ for the inverse roots of the numerator of $Z(C,t)$ without invoking Weil’s theorem, Riemann–Roch on $C\times C$ or the Hodge index theorem.

**Proposed by.** [S03].

**Background.** [NS18]; [DMPR21]; [Del74].

**Solved when.** A definition satisfying (i)–(iii), with the test of Criterion 57.5 passed for every curve over a finite field. The bound $|\alpha_i|=\sqrt q$ is a theorem (Weil); the problem is to obtain it from the new structure.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Work on Real topological Hochschild homology and its cyclotomic structure was found [DMPR21]; no notion of polarization of the required kind was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 36](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/36). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
