# Problem 26 · Sharp accumulation bounds for equivariance defects

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 6 ([A chain of non-implications](../THESES.md#thesis-6)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], Question 11.11.18, p. 2259. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** X (open question).

**Statement.** The accumulation bound of [S02, Theorem 11.9.28] for the equivariance defects of successive layers is tight in the worst case, but its use as a certificate needs a lower bound on output norms that is not available. Question 11.11.18 asks: “Is there a class of architectures with certified lower norm control on which a usable ratio of bound to witness is O(1)? Failing that, is there a sharper bound that tracks the direction of the equivariance defect rather than only its magnitude, for instance by carrying a covariance rather than a scalar?”

**What the source establishes.** “Theorem 11.9.28 is tight in the worst case, with both of its constants attained.” Without the lower-norm premise the gate leaves the claim open: “A witness may still falsify a claim but cannot replace the missing certificate.” The source suggests, without attempting it, that defects may add in quadrature under a randomness assumption.

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** Error accumulation in composed computations [Hig02]; equivariance in deep learning [BBCV21].

**Solved when.** Either a class of architectures with certified lower norm control on which the ratio of bound to witness is proved O(1), or a direction-sensitive bound strictly sharper than Theorem 11.9.28 on a stated class, with proof.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No result of this form for equivariance defects was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 26](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/26). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
