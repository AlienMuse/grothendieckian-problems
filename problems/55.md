# Problem 55 · Ambidexterity over the arithmetic base

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, programme level. Thesis 8 ([The rising sea](../THESES.md#thesis-8)).

**Source.** *The Arithmetic Topos* [S07], Problem 40.11, p. 1603. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23629681](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23629681).

**Status in the source.** CONJECTURE.

**Statement.** Let $q\colon\mathrm{Bun}_G(\mathfrak X_{\mathbb Z})\to\mathrm{pt}$ be the structure map. Prove or refute that $q_!\simeq q_*$ on $\mathrm{Shv}_{\mathcal N}(\mathrm{Bun}_G(\mathfrak X_{\mathbb Z}))$ after Whittaker renormalisation; if the equivalence holds only after a renormalisation, identify it explicitly and determine whether it is canonical.

**What the source establishes.** “This is the load-bearing hypothesis of the entire framework.” Over a curve the corresponding statement, miraculous duality, is a theorem of Drinfeld and Gaitsgory; over $\mathfrak X_{\mathbb Z}$ the local finiteness its proof uses is unavailable. “It is also the gap about which the least can currently be said.”

**Proposed by.** [S07]; over a curve the source cites miraculous duality as a theorem of Drinfeld and Gaitsgory; see [Gai18] and [AGK+20].

**Background.** [DG15]; [Gai18]; [AGK+20].

**Solved when.** A proof or a refutation of the equivalence, with the renormalisation identified if one is needed.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Miraculous duality and duality for automorphic sheaves over curves were found [Gai18; AGK+20]; no arithmetic analogue was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 55](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/55). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
