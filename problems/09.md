# Problem 9 · The seven obligations of descent execution

**Kind.** Formalization problem, research level. Thesis 5 ([Report failure; abstain without evidence](../THESES.md#thesis-5)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. III, §56.15, pp. 9130–9132, and §56.1, p. 9119. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** O.

**Statement.** The Grothendieckian Descent Reasoning Architecture (GDRA) of [S01], the layer that executes local-to-global campaigns, exists to prevent one error: “The central architectural error is to convert operational completion into global meaning.” Give a formal transition model of GDRA campaigns and prove the seven properties that §56.15 leaves open: “canonical serialization must preserve identity; every accepted transition must preserve the invariants; forbidden authority effects must be noninterfering; budget events must conserve declared dimensions; event replay must converge to the same eligible head under the stated delivery model; counterevidence closure must be omission-resistant; and adapters must preserve the disjoint failure constructors.”

**What the source establishes.** The source states the seven properties and the delivery model (at-least-once delivery with idempotent effects), and records: “None has a supplied mechanized proof.”

**Proposed by.** [S01].

**Background.** Noninterference [GM82]; linearizability [HW90].

**Solved when.** Machine-checked proofs of all seven properties for a model whose correspondence with Chapter 56 is documented clause by clause, or a machine-checked counterexample to one of them.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Noninterference and replay-safety proofs exist for other systems; none for this model was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 9](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/9). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
