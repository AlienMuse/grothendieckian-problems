# Problem 15 · A preregistered test of typed base change

**Kind.** Machine problem, entry level. Thesis 1 ([Worlds, not isolated theorems](../THESES.md#thesis-1)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. I, §11.12, p. 277 (Conjecture V1-CH-011-C001); definitions, pp. 275–276. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** C (conjecture).

**Statement.** Test the conjecture of [S01, Vol. I, §11.12]: “For a corpus whose categorical claims are already expressed as finite typed diagrams, requiring an RCP/RCT pair for each use of “base change” will reduce orientation and hypothesis errors relative to an untyped prose baseline.” Here an RCP (relative change passport) records the bases, the base arrow and its variance, and an RCT (relative claim trace) records the status and hypotheses of the claim made after the change (Definitions V1-CH-011-D003 and D004).

**What the source establishes.** “This is a falsifiable editorial-engineering conjecture. It requires a preregistered corpus, error taxonomy, reviewers, and comparison protocol; no performance result is claimed here.”

**Proposed by.** [S01].

**Background.** Preregistration [NEDM18].

**Solved when.** A study whose corpus, error taxonomy, reviewer assignment and analysis are registered before data are collected, with independent reviewers, reporting the result in either direction.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No study of this question was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 15](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/15). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
