# Problem 39 · A certified inertia for the pole-removed Weil form

**Kind.** Machine problem, entry level. Thesis 9 ([State what is not established](../THESES.md#thesis-9)).

**Source.** *Arithmetic Spectral Descent* [S03], Criterion 58.9 (falsification predicate F2), p. 1169; Proposition 46.5 and Remark 46.13, pp. xxxv, 919. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23564898](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23564898).

**Status in the source.** E (finite measurement) / O.

**Statement.** For sixty-three cubic-spline test spaces $V_{T,N}$ ($0.15\le T\le 3$, $4\le N\le 64$) the source measured, in floating point with tolerance $10^{-9}$, that the pole-removed Weil form $B_N=W_N-P_N$ has inertia $(N-1,1)$. Certify this measurement in interval or exact arithmetic for each configuration — certify $N-1$ positive eigenvalues and exhibit a vector $z$ with a certified enclosure of $B_N(z,z)<0$ — or show for some configuration that the certified inertia differs, which is falsification predicate F2 of [S03, Criterion 58.9].

**What the source establishes.** Under RH the full Weil form is positive semidefinite and $n_-(B_N)\le1$ (Corollary 46.11); the lower bound $n_-(B_N)\ge1$ is not proved conditionally or otherwise (Warning 46.12). The measurement is finite evidence: “What the data exclude is a large second negative direction. What they do not exclude is a small one.” F2 asks for “the computation certified in interval arithmetic”.

**Proposed by.** [S03].

**Background.** Weil positivity at the archimedean place [CC21]; interval arithmetic [Joh17]; certified computation of zeta zeros [PT21]; certification for other finite truncations of the Weil form [Gro26].

**Solved when.** Certified inertia for all sixty-three configurations of Proposition 46.5, with the code and the enclosures published; a configuration whose certified inertia is not $(N-1,1)$ also solves the problem, by firing F2. The result is a statement about finitely many matrices; it is not evidence for or against RH.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** A certification rule and an interval factorization for the Galerkin truncations of the Weil quadratic form of Connes, van Suijlekom, Consani and Moscovici were found [Gro26]; no certified computation of the inertia of the Gram matrices of these sixty-three test spaces was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 39](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/39). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
