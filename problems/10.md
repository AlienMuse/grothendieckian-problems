# Problem 10 · Non-self-authorization

**Kind.** Formalization problem, research level. Thesis 7 ([Machines propose; humans authorize](../THESES.md#thesis-7)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. V, Proposition 1.3, p. 11913; Proposition 5.1, p. 11940; Proposition 10.1, p. 11979. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** D/O (definition; obligation).

**Statement.** The rule that machines propose and humans authorise publication is stated in [S01, Vol. V] as three propositions, each conditional on assumptions about capabilities. Proposition 1.3 concludes that “a non-root module cannot, through a conforming Volume V interface, make its own output both authoritative and active”; Proposition 10.1 that “adding a validated evidence edge to an artifact cannot by itself increase the artifact’s epistemic class”. Give a formal model of capabilities, signing and status transitions in which the hypotheses of Propositions 1.3, 5.1 and 10.1 are definitions, and prove the three conclusions.

**What the source establishes.** The propositions are argued informally and marked D/O: “The proposition is conditional on correct capability isolation and validator independence; those conditions are test obligations, not assumptions to be hidden.” The negative cases (a worker writing to the status store, a signature detached from the artifact hash) are listed.

**Proposed by.** [S01].

**Background.** [DV66]; [SS75]; [GM82].

**Solved when.** Machine-checked proofs of the three propositions in a published model, and a test suite showing that each listed negative case is rejected by an implementation conforming to the model.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Verified capability machines and separation kernels exist; no formalisation of these three propositions was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 10](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/10). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
