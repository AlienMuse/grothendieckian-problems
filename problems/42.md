# Problem 42 · Sub-conductor rank detection

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 12 ([Judge a method by its open ledger](../THESES.md#thesis-12)).

**Source.** *Fractured Adelic Geometry* [S04], Question 46.2.1, p. 354. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23585887](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23585887).

**Status in the source.** OPEN.

**Statement.** Does there exist a natural family $\mathcal F_X$ of elliptic curves, a function $P(X)=X^{o(1)}$, and explicit statistics $S_X$ depending only on $\{a_p(E): p\le P(X)\}$, such that the conditional laws of $S_X$ for analytic rank $0$, $1$ and at least $2$ have distinct non-degenerate limits after the root number and all known local confounders are matched?

**What the source establishes.** The source explains the clause on confounders: “The matching clause matters. Without it, a classifier may merely learn the functional-equation sign or a database convention.” Either answer is informative: “A positive answer would ask for a new family trace formula; a negative answer would also be informative by locating the finite-sample origin of the observed signal.”

**Proposed by.** [S04].

**Background.** [HLOP22]; [Zub23]; [BKLPR15]; neural rank prediction from Frobenius traces [BCD+26].

**Solved when.** A theorem establishing distinct limits for a stated family and statistic with $P(X)=X^{o(1)}$, or a theorem that no statistic of this kind separates the ranks after matching.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Machine-learning prediction of analytic rank from Frobenius traces has been reported with high accuracy [BCD+26] and murmurations studied [HLOP22; Zub23]; no theorem of the requested form, with the matching clause, was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 42](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/42). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
