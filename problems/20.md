# Problem 20 · Nerve baselines between GRPO and RLOO

**Kind.** Machine problem, research level. Thesis 4 ([Attention as descent](../THESES.md#thesis-4)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], §1.2.4, pp. 21–22 (claim C11 of Appendix A7). DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** open empirical question.

**Statement.** The source proves that the group-relative advantage of group relative policy optimisation (GRPO) [SWZ+24] equals $\Delta_0 R/\lVert\delta_0 R\rVert_2$ for the $0$-Laplacian of the nerve of the sampling group, wherever $\sigma>0$. This places GRPO in “a family of graph baselines indexed by a nerve, of which the complete-graph member is GRPO and the i.i.d. leave-one-out member is RLOO.” (RLOO is the leave-one-out REINFORCE baseline [ACG+24].) Determine whether another member of the family reduces the variance of the policy-gradient estimate at matched cost.

**What the source establishes.** The identity is proved and the family defined; “Whether any other member of that family reduces variance at matched cost is an open empirical question, tested by claim C11 of A7 and unanswered here.”

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** [SWZ+24]; [ACG+24]; recent baselines for reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards [ZZA+25].

**Solved when.** A theorem identifying, under stated assumptions on rewards, a nerve whose baseline has strictly smaller variance than both GRPO and RLOO at matched sampling cost, or proving that no member of the family does; or the registered experiment C11 with its outcome.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Variance-reduction baselines for GRPO-type methods are an active topic [ZZA+25]; no study of the nerve family was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 20](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/20). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
