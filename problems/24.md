# Problem 24 · Compiled-to-streaming descent stability

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 4 ([Attention as descent](../THESES.md#thesis-4)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], Conjecture 2.2.15, p. 104. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** D/OPEN.

**Statement.** Consider a session tower of finite certified sites and solid-valued states $(\mathcal C_n,P_n,F_n)_{n\ge 0}$ with the site maps of [S02, §2.2]. Conjecture 2.2.15: “If the cd-certificates are uniformly compatible under these site morphisms, the density bound is uniform, the coefficient towers satisfy the required derived-limit vanishing condition, and the finite probe systems form a conservative filtered family, then the limit state satisfies Nisnevich descent with solid condensed coefficients.” Prove or refute it.

**What the source establishes.** “The hypotheses are not discharged.” The source explains which hypotheses fail in practice — “Uniform density generally fails when new maximal atoms are added; derived-limit vanishing is not certified by the runtime; and no conservative finite probe family has been proved for arbitrary state classes” — and that “The conjecture therefore cannot be used to upgrade stagewise FINITE-ONLY certificates to a session-wide theorem.”

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** Nisnevich descent [MV99]; condensed and solid mathematics [Sch19]; [Lur09].

**Solved when.** A proof of the conjecture as stated, or a counterexample tower satisfying the four hypotheses whose limit state fails Nisnevich descent.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Work on descent for condensed and syntomic coefficients was found; no result on limits of towers of finite certified sites was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 24](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/24). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
