# Problem 43 · Murmurations from the flow: a falsification test

**Kind.** Machine problem, entry level. Thesis 9 ([State what is not established](../THESES.md#thesis-9)).

**Source.** *Fractured Adelic Geometry* [S04], Conjecture 36.4.6, pp. 276–277. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23585887](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23585887).

**Status in the source.** OPEN (a falsifiable prediction).

**Statement.** If the framework of [S04] is correct, the murmuration density arises as a transition term in its Lefschetz–Verdier localisation, and three computable predictions follow: (i) $y=p/N$ is the natural coordinate, and no other combination of $p$ and $N$ appears in the leading term; (ii) the density for elliptic curves, if it exists, has the same oscillation frequencies as the modular-form density $M_2$; (iii) the rank-conditioned densities are governed by the same density with the root number replaced by the Selmer fixed-point class. Carry out the tests.

**What the source establishes.** “Each clause is falsifiable by computation. Clause (i) fails if any family shows a leading term depending on p and N other than through p/N; clause (ii) fails if the observed elliptic-curve frequencies differ from those of M2.” The protocol is in Chapter 55.

**Proposed by.** [S04].

**Background.** [HLOP22]; [Zub23]; [SS25]; [Cow24].

**Solved when.** The tests of clauses (i) and (ii) carried out under the protocol of [S04, Ch. 55] against the densities of [Zub23] and [SS25], with data and code published and the outcome reported in either direction; clause (iii) when the Selmer class is computable.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** A conjectural explicit murmuration density for elliptic curves ordered by height has been given [SS25], and murmurations have been derived from ratios conjectures [Cow24]; these supply what clause (ii) is tested against. No test of the three clauses was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 43](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/43). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
