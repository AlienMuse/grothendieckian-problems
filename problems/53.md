# Problem 53 · The multiplicative-group gate

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 9 ([State what is not established](../THESES.md#thesis-9)).

**Source.** *The Arithmetic Topos* [S07], Criterion 1.26, p. 28. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23629681](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23629681).

**Status in the source.** FORMAL TARGET.

**Statement.** Let $\mathrm{Bun}^{\mathrm{ar}}_{\mathbb G_m}$ be the arithmetic Picard stack of the global arithmetic curve $\mathfrak X_{\mathbb Z}$ of [S07] and $\mathrm{Gauge}$ its category of gauge sheaves. Construct them and prove or refute that $\zeta(\mathrm{TC}(\Phi\mid\mathrm{Gauge}(\mathrm{Bun}^{\mathrm{ar}}_{\mathbb G_m})),s)=\pi^{-s/2} \Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s)$, the completed Riemann zeta function, with the archimedean factor produced by the archimedean chart and not inserted.

**What the source establishes.** The source calls this “the primary go/no-go test for the framework”: “If the framework yields anything else — in particular, if it yields 𝜁(𝑠) without the Γ-factor, or with a Γ-factor of the wrong argument — then it is wrong, and no amount of repair at higher strata will save it.” “This criterion is designated for formal verification; its current status is recorded in Appendix F.”

**Proposed by.** [S07].

**Background.** Archimedean factors as regularized determinants [Den98]; [CC21]; [NS18].

**Solved when.** A construction of the objects in the abelian case and a proof of the displayed identity, or a proof that the construction yields a different function, which refutes the framework as stated. The completed zeta function and its functional equation are classical; the problem is whether this construction produces them.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No construction of the arithmetic Picard stack of this framework was found outside [S07].

**Discussion.** [Issue 53](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/53). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
