# Problem 19 · The capability wager

**Kind.** Machine problem, research level. Thesis 4 ([Attention as descent](../THESES.md#thesis-4)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], Conjecture 3.6.17, p. 778; §1.2.5, p. 22. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** D (design-grade conjecture).

**Statement.** On an orthogonal cellular sheaf the non-abelian holonomy capacity $\beta$ of [S02] is either $0$ or at least a constant $c(d)$ depending only on the stalk dimension. The wager is that $\beta$ governs capability. In the words of the source: “At matched parameters, data and compute, compare models with CERTIFIED_VIRTUALLY_ABELIAN and CERTIFIED_FREE reports on tasks requiring route-dependent evidence.” Decide the wager.

**What the source establishes.** The dichotomy is proved; the wager is not. “That β governs capability is grade D and we have no proof. It is the most interesting claim in the document and the least supported.” The comparison is set up so that a negative result costs only the wager: “No theorem links the verdict to capability; failure of the registered comparison removes the capability claim while leaving the group theorem intact.”

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** The Tits alternative [Tit72]; uniform growth gaps [Bre11]; [HG19].

**Solved when.** A preregistered comparison at matched parameters, data and compute, with certificates computed as in [S02, Ch. 3.6], published with its outcome in either direction.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No study linking holonomy growth of cellular sheaves to model capability was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 19](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/19). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
