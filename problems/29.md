# Problem 29 · The training-to-serving deviation

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 6 ([A chain of non-implications](../THESES.md#thesis-6)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], Table 13.16.13, entry O16, pp. 3174–3175. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** open.

**Statement.** Prove that “the training-to-serving deviation is bounded” for the forward map of [S02, Ch. 13.10–13.11], or remove the failure: the register asks for “a proved bound, or a tie-break that removes the failure”.

**What the source establishes.** Bitwise equality between training and serving is proved under stated contracts and fails outside them: “equality fails through the tie-break under rounding; no bound on the output difference is proved”.

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** [IEEE19]; [Hig02]; deterministic inference across parallel configurations [ZDY+25].

**Solved when.** A proved bound on the output difference between training and serving under the rounding model of Chapter 13.11, or a tie-break rule with a proof that it restores equality.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Engineering work on deterministic inference was found [ZDY+25]; no proved bound for this forward map was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 29](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/29). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
