# Problem 37 · Construction Problem A1 on the syntomic stack

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 8 ([The rising sea](../THESES.md#thesis-8)).

**Source.** *Arithmetic Spectral Descent* [S03], Problem 22.9, p. 483. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23564898](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23564898).

**Status in the source.** O.

**Statement.** Construct, on quasi-coherent sheaves on the syntomic stack $\mathbb Z_p^{\mathrm{syn}}$ (equivalently on $F$-gauges over $\mathbb Z_p$), a $\mathbb C$-linear endomorphism $\Theta$ of the underlying object after $\otimes_{W,\iota}\mathbb C$ such that (A1.1) on the Hodge–Tate locus $\Theta=-\tfrac{2\pi i}{\log q}\Theta_{\mathrm{Sen}}$ for objects pulled back from $\mathbb F_q$; (A1.2) $q^\Theta=\mathrm{Fr}_q^*$ for $F$-gauges coming from smooth proper $X/\mathbb F_q$; (A1.3) $\Theta$ is $\mathbb C$-linear, which “rules out semilinear operators”; and (A1.4) the regularized determinant $\det_\infty(s-\Theta)$ on motivic cohomology converges and equals $\zeta(X,s)$.

**What the source establishes.** The source isolates the difficulty in (A1.3): the Frobenius part of $\Theta$ is semilinear over $W$, and over $\mathbb Z_p$ no power of $\varphi$ is $W$-linear. “The syntomic stack exists, but the operator on it does not—yet.”

**Proposed by.** [S03].

**Background.** [BS22]; [BL22]; [Dri20].

**Solved when.** A construction satisfying (A1.1)–(A1.4), or a proof that no endomorphism with (A1.1)–(A1.3) exists.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** The Sen operator on the Hodge–Tate stack is known [BL22; Dri20]; no operator with (A1.1)–(A1.4) was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 37](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/37). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
