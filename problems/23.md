# Problem 23 · A defence against a globally coherent falsehood

**Kind.** Machine problem, programme level. Thesis 5 ([Report failure; abstain without evidence](../THESES.md#thesis-5)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], §1.2.5, p. 22. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** open (“we have no structural defence to propose”).

**Statement.** “Descent verification provably catches local inconsistency. Against a globally coherent falsehood it offers nothing, and we have no structural defence to propose.” Give a verification method that detects a stated class of globally coherent falsehoods, or prove that no verifier with access only to stated information can do so.

**What the source establishes.** The source proves the local guarantee and records the global gap as one of “The five things we are least sure of”; Part VI of [S02] discusses reward hacking at length without proposing a defence.

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** [SHKK22]; selective prediction [EW10]; [FKKG24].

**Solved when.** Either a method with a theorem stating which globally coherent falsehoods it detects and at what cost, evaluated on a published adversarial benchmark, or an impossibility theorem for a stated class of verifiers.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Consistency- and verification-based hallucination detection was found; no structural defence or impossibility theorem for globally coherent falsehoods was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 23](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/23). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
