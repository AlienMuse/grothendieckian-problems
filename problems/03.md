# Problem 3 · From obstruction to conjecture

**Kind.** Machine problem, research level. Thesis 10 ([Discovery is recursive](../THESES.md#thesis-10)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. IV, §109.1, p. 11670, and §109.5, p. 11675 (algorithm ALG-118). DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** ALG-R (research algorithm).

**Statement.** Implement the obstruction-driven conjecture generator ALG-118 of [S01, Vol. IV, Ch. 109], which takes replayable, typed records of failed constructions or proof attempts and returns scoped conjectures, each with at least one executable falsifier, and run it until it produces a conjecture that is then settled by others.

**What the source establishes.** The source specifies the contract and its limits: “An obstruction can explain why a particular construction, proof route, or finite experiment failed. It does not by itself establish the negation, necessity, naturalness, or novelty of a conjecture inferred from that failure.” Status is not affected by how a candidate looks: “Frequency, linguistic plausibility, retrieval rank, model agreement, elegance, and apparent usefulness do not change its epistemic status.” Falsifiers are part of the output, and “No finite search can certify an unrestricted conjecture.” No implementation exists.

**Proposed by.** [S01].

**Background.** [Lak76]; automated conjecturing [Faj88; RGM+21]; [DVB+21].

**Solved when.** An implementation run on a published set of typed obstructions, with the full record of every candidate and of its falsifiers, such that at least one generated conjecture that is not in the frozen corpus (in the scoped sense of [S01, Vol. IV, §110.8]) is afterwards proved, or refuted by a replayed counterexample, by parties independent of the generator.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Theory exploration and conjecturing systems were found [Faj88; RGM+21]; none takes typed obstruction records as input or attaches falsifiers to each output.

**Discussion.** [Issue 3](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/3). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
