# Problem 35 · The computational-univalence bridge

**Kind.** Formalization problem, programme level. Thesis 4 ([Attention as descent](../THESES.md#thesis-4)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], Hypothesis 4.7.26, p. 1103. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** X.

**Statement.** Build the checker of Hypothesis CU-MJ of [S02]: “There exists an approved external checker that: 1. represents proof-relevant paths and a univalent universe independently of Lean equality; 2. computes coercion along equivalences using cubical composition or an equivalent certified mechanism; 3. checks the higher coherences required by the transported family; 4. emits a replayable term rather than a Boolean success flag;” and 5. binds that term to the canonical target, equivalence, site, cover, universe and checker of the justification being transported.

**What the source establishes.** “No such integrated component is supplied by this chapter, so CU-MJ is graded X.” Every theorem that assumes it is conditional; the finite branch of the chapter does not need it.

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** [CCHM18]; [VMA21]; [Uni13].

**Solved when.** A checker satisfying the five clauses, and one transported justification from [S02, Ch. 4.7] checked end to end with its replayable term published.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Cubical proof assistants and proof-transfer tools exist [CCHM18; VMA21]; no checker bound to Lean targets in the sense of clause 5 was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 35](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/35). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
