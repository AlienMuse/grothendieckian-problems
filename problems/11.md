# Problem 11 · Evolution without authority

**Kind.** Formalization problem, research level. Thesis 7 ([Machines propose; humans authorize](../THESES.md#thesis-7)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. VI, §11.11, p. 13204; Vol. III, §21.16, item 12, pp. 8370–8371. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** O (obligation O-V6-11-04).

**Statement.** A self-improving component may propose changes to itself but must not acquire authority. In [S01, Vol. VI] the obligation reads: “O-V6-11-04 — prove that no composition of EvolutionContract ports reaches signing, promotion, activation, root mutation, or rollback capabilities”. Prove it for a formal model of the evolution interface.

**What the source establishes.** In the released architecture “Evolution remains inert: telemetry cannot evaluate candidates, adapt routing, update policy, canary, promote, deploy, or roll back.” The source does not claim more: “Until these obligations are discharged, policy morphisms and version graphs are precise governance objects, not a claim that safe whole-system evolution has been solved.”

**Proposed by.** [S01].

**Background.** [SS75]; reward hacking as the failure this obligation guards against [SHKK22].

**Solved when.** A machine-checked reachability theorem over all finite compositions of the ports of the published EvolutionContract model, with the model’s correspondence to Vol. VI, Ch. 11 documented; or a composition that reaches a forbidden capability.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Recent work studies privilege escalation in AI agents empirically; no formal reachability proof for an evolution interface of this kind was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 11](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/11). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
