# Problem 56 · Construct the global arithmetic curve

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, programme level. Thesis 8 ([The rising sea](../THESES.md#thesis-8)).

**Source.** *The Arithmetic Topos* [S07], Problem 40.10, p. 1602. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23629681](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23629681).

**Status in the source.** CONJECTURE.

**Statement.** Exhibit a condensed analytic stack over the sphere spectrum satisfying the eight clauses of the definition of the global arithmetic curve $\mathfrak X_{\mathbb Z}$ [S07, Definition 10.15] — finite syntomic charts, an archimedean chart, a restricted adelic halo with a rational diagonal, the gluing as a homotopy fibre product, an absolute Frobenius, a gauge map to $\mathbb A^1/\mathbb G_m$, a place map to $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z$, and a fundamental class in degree three — prove it unique up to canonical equivalence, and equip it with a six-functor formalism with proper base change and Verdier duality.

**What the source establishes.** “What exists at present is a specification and two of its charts.” The finite charts are theorems of Bhatt–Lurie and Drinfeld; the archimedean chart is stipulated. “The obstacle is not the charts. It is the gluing, and the reason is specific. Adelic descent along a restricted product is not a standard operation in any existing formalism.”

**Proposed by.** [S07].

**Background.** [BL22]; [Dri20]; [BL95]; [FS21]; [Sch19].

**Solved when.** A construction with the eight clauses, the uniqueness statement and the six-functor formalism, or a proof that no object satisfies the eight clauses.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No construction of this kind was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 56](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/56). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
