# Problem 38 · Archimedean Frobenius from quaternionic THH

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 8 ([The rising sea](../THESES.md#thesis-8)).

**Source.** *Arithmetic Spectral Descent* [S03], Problem 28.13, p. 604; Construction 28.14, p. 605. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23564898](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23564898).

**Status in the source.** O (first half answered since; see the literature check).

**Statement.** Problem 28.13 of [S03] asks two questions. The first — whether there is “a lift of THH to genuine Pin(2)-spectra rather than O(2)-spectra” — has been answered: quaternionic topological Hochschild homology THQ, defined for rings with twisted $C_4$-action, carries a $\mathrm{Pin}(2)$-action [AMP26]. The second remains: is its $\mathrm{Pin}(2)$-homotopy fixed-point theory “the correct 𝜑∞ for symplectically self-dual motives, with 𝑂(2) handling the orthogonal ones?” Concretely, determine whether $\mathrm{THQ}(R)^{h\mathrm{Pin}(2)}$ carries an operator $\Theta_\infty$ satisfying the analogue of requirement (C′1) of Construction 28.14 — its half-line spectrum, with Hodge multiplicities, gives the archimedean local factors of symplectically self-dual motives by $\det_\infty((s-\Theta_\infty)/2\pi)$.

**What the source establishes.** The source calls the question “a well-posed question in equivariant stable homotopy theory, independent of RH”, and states that “No existing construction is known”; that statement is corrected here (it is recorded in the errata of the repository). Construction 28.14 lists the requirements (C′1)–(C′4) for the orthogonal case.

**Proposed by.** [S03].

**Background.** [AMP26]; [DMPR21]; [Man16]; [NS18].

**Solved when.** An operator on $\mathrm{THQ}(R)^{h\mathrm{Pin}(2)}$ for a stated class of rings with the required structure, with a proof of the analogue of (C′1) for symplectically self-dual motives in that class; or a proof that no natural operator can satisfy it.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Found: Angelini-Knoll, Merling and Péroux construct THQ with its Pin(2)-action (arXiv:2409.18187, 2024; Adv. Math. 2026) [AMP26]. This answers the first question of Problem 28.13; no work on the second was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 38](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/38). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
