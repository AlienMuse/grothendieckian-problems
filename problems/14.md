# Problem 14 · The language forge, end to end

**Kind.** Machine problem, programme level. Thesis 11 ([Creating language](../THESES.md#thesis-11)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. III, Preface, p. 7956; §43.14, p. 8873; §44.16, pp. 8888–8889; §45.8, p. 8898. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** O.

**Statement.** “The language forge follows a deliberately articulated cycle: collect, mine, name, notate, ground, reconstruct, axiomatize, grow, re-encode, and validate.” Run the cycle of [S01, Vol. III, Ch. 41–48] end to end on a published corpus and produce a new notation and language package for a declared mathematical domain, with its metatheoretic obligations discharged.

**What the source establishes.** The source specifies each stage and leaves completeness open: “The order organizes a bounded language-formation workflow and leaves completeness as an open research question.” It requires that “Notation earns trust by declaring what it compresses, what it preserves, and what it cannot recover”, and that “An obligation is not promoted because it is conventional for a language family or appears plausible from examples.”

**Proposed by.** [S01].

**Background.** [Gan13]; [CF09].

**Solved when.** A package for a declared domain in which every introduced primitive is linked to the structural motifs and counterexamples that motivated it, whose substitution, preservation and conservativity obligations (§44.16) are machine-checked, and whose round trips (Ch. 43) pass on a held-out set of statements from the domain.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Work on mathematical concept formation and on the language of mathematics [Gan13] was found; no end-to-end language-formation system with these obligations was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 14](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/14). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
