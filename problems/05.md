# Problem 5 · Target fidelity

**Kind.** Machine problem, research level. Thesis 6 ([A chain of non-implications](../THESES.md#thesis-6)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. III, §30.10, p. 8574, and §64.17, p. 9294; Architectural Prospectus, p. 7966; Vol. IV, §5.16, p. 9846. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** O (obligation).

**Statement.** A proof kernel checks a proof against a formal statement; it does not check that the formal statement is the mathematics it was meant to express. In the source: “A named, pinned formal kernel may check a proof term against a formal target; target fidelity and publication authority remain separate obligations.” Give a certificate format and checker for the fidelity of a formal statement to a statement in a source text, within a declared fragment of mathematical language and a declared library.

**What the source establishes.** The source treats every machine proof as a candidate “until source-target fidelity, kernel replay, and axiom-closure review pass under the same canonical subject key”, and lists among the open obligations of the formal layer to “verify that adapted definitions and rule candidates faithfully reflect the cited locators and declared deltas” and “target-fidelity theorems connecting source constructs to generated declarations”.

**Proposed by.** [S01]; the question is shared with the autoformalization literature [WJL+22; FLM+26].

**Background.** [BW05]; [WJL+22]; misformalization records in [FLM+26].

**Solved when.** A certificate format that binds a formal target, a source locator and a declared map of definitions, and a checker that rejects every certificate in a published adversarial suite of statement pairs with deliberately introduced misformalizations (quantifier order, hypothesis loss, changed definitions) while accepting the faithful pairs, with the suite, the checker and the results released for independent replay.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Many 2025–2026 preprints measure faithfulness of autoformalization by scores or round-trip tests; none found issues a fidelity certificate of the kind required here.

**Discussion.** [Issue 5](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/5). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
