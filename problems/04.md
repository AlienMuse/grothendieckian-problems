# Problem 4 · Minimal extension questions

**Kind.** Machine problem, research level. Thesis 1 ([Worlds, not isolated theorems](../THESES.md#thesis-1)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. III, §24.10, pp. 8440–8441. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** O (obligation).

**Statement.** “MinimalExtensionQuestion turns a diagnosed deficit into an ordered search over semantic deltas.” For a declared class of mathematical worlds and a declared partial order on deltas, give a procedure that, from an independently checked obstruction, returns an extension that is minimal for that order, consistent, conservative over the predecessor world, and reopens the blocked obligation; or prove that no such procedure exists for the class.

**What the source establishes.** The question belongs to the Topos New-Creation Architecture (TNCA), the engine of [S01] that proposes successor worlds. The source fixes the meaning of minimality — “Minimality is relative to that declared partial order, not to implementation effort or textual brevity” — and gives the two failure modes it must avoid (jumping to a stronger logic, and refusing an extension that is needed). It states what is missing: “TNCA has not shown existence, consistency, conservativity, or usefulness of any answer.”

**Proposed by.** [S01].

**Background.** Conservative extensions in logic and type theory; [Mac78] for the categorical constructions involved.

**Solved when.** A stated class of worlds (for example finitely axiomatised first-order theories or finite presentations of categories), a declared order on deltas, and a machine-checked proof that the procedure is correct on the class, with an implementation evaluated on published obstructions; or a machine-checked impossibility result for a class of comparable generality.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Work on conservative extension and minimal extensions in logic was found; no procedure with the inputs and guarantees of §24.10 was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 4](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/4). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
