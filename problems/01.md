# Problem 1 · The first rising-sea event

**Kind.** Machine problem, programme level. Thesis 8 ([The rising sea](../THESES.md#thesis-8)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. III, §5.1, p. 8034, and §5.8, p. 8039. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** S/R (source-grounded synthesis; falsifiable research construction).

**Statement.** Carry out one operational rising-sea event in the sense of [S01, Vol. III, §5.1]. Such an event “must begin from an explicit obstruction or language deficit, propose a typed extension, compile bounded probes, preserve or explicitly surrender recovery, record costs and residuals, and submit any status request to authority outside the producing engines.” The problem asks for an event of this kind after which the original difficulty, stated before the event, has become tractable in the extended setting without the question having been changed.

**What the source establishes.** The source fixes the sequence obstruction → typed extension → bounded execution and checking → computed, partial or failed → recovery and transport → external request, observes that “Each arrow may fail”, and declines to promote the strategy: “Volume III does not turn that strategy into a universal theorem of problem solving. It turns it into a falsifiable campaign record.” It names four ways in which an extension changes the question instead of answering it: an extension that “deletes the predicate expressing a contradiction, forgets the invariant detecting failure, weakens equality until disagreement disappears, or renames an unknown as a new primitive has not resolved the original obstruction.” Section 5.8 specifies the residual and non-equivalence records that a partial outcome must leave. No event has been carried out.

**Proposed by.** The image of the rising sea is Grothendieck’s [McL07]; its operational form and this problem are from [S01].

**Background.** [McL07]; [Lak76]; machine-guided discovery with an external evaluator [DVB+21; RBN+24].

**Solved when.** A campaign record is published that a third party can replay, in which (i) every step of the §5.1 sequence is present as a typed artifact; (ii) the target statement, frozen before the campaign, receives in the extended setting a kernel-checked proof, a kernel-checked refutation, or a reduction to named hypotheses with their statuses recorded; and (iii) a reviewer independent of the producing system certifies that none of the four failures of §5.1 applies. A complete record of a failed campaign is listed as a partial result.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No published campaign record of this form was found. Machine-guided discovery systems [DVB+21; RBN+24; TWL+24] evaluate candidates externally but do not record the four failure patterns or a separate status request.

**Discussion.** [Issue 1](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/1). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
