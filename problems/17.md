# Problem 17 · A preregistered test of obstruction records

**Kind.** Machine problem, entry level. Thesis 5 ([Report failure; abstain without evidence](../THESES.md#thesis-5)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. I, Ch. 17, p. 404 (Conjecture V1-CH-017-C001). DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** C.

**Statement.** Test the conjecture of [S01, Vol. I, Ch. 17]: “For a fixed benchmark of finite, fully enumerated lifting and gluing problems with independently verified ground truth, requiring the fields of ObstructionRecordV1 will reduce false “no solution” classifications relative to an otherwise identical checker that stores only a Boolean success flag.”

**What the source establishes.** “This is an empirical systems conjecture, not a mathematical theorem.” The same section forbids the strengthening that every failed construction determines a cohomology class whose vanishing solves the problem.

**Proposed by.** [S01].

**Background.** [NEDM18]; [Gir71] for obstruction classes.

**Solved when.** A preregistered comparison of the two checkers on a published benchmark with enumerated ground truth, reporting the false-negative rates of both with confidence intervals.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No study of this question was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 17](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/17). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
