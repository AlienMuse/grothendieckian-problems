# Problem 32 · Second-order disagreement without engineering

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 5 ([Report failure; abstain without evidence](../THESES.md#thesis-5)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], §1.1.13, p. 15; §1.2.5, p. 22; §9.1.7, p. 1766. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** open.

**Statement.** “On a graph, and under standard top-k routing, H2 vanishes identically.” The degree-two (gerbe) theory of disagreement among experts is therefore vacuous unless the cover is built for it: “The minimal non-vacuous configuration is four experts arranged as ∂Δ3, and it must be engineered deliberately.” Determine whether covers produced by a data-driven routing or cover-construction procedure, on natural tasks, carry non-zero second cohomology with the coefficient data of [S02].

**What the source establishes.** The source proves the vanishing results and warns that “engineering a structure into existence in order to measure it is a move that deserves suspicion.” A gerbe diagnostic is “vacuous unless the cover has a deliberate, data-justified sparse nerve with genuine two-dimensional cycles and explicitly defined coefficient data.”

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** [Gir71]; [HG19].

**Solved when.** A procedure, not tuned to produce two-cycles, whose covers on a published natural task carry a certified non-zero class in degree two; or a theorem that a stated class of procedures always yields vanishing second cohomology.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No result on second cohomology of routing nerves was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 32](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/32). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
