# Problem 48 · A prism reading of Voisin’s counterexample

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, entry level. Thesis 9 ([State what is not established](../THESES.md#thesis-9)).

**Source.** *The Archimedean Prism* [S05], Problem 71.6.6, p. 1025; dossier, p. 1031. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23597954](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23597954).

**Status in the source.** open.

**Statement.** “Compute the prism obstruction class of Voisin’s 2002 Weil-torus counterexample and verify that it is exactly the projectivity obstruction, not a new one.”

**What the source establishes.** “Theorem 37.2.4 predicts the counterexample strikes at the projectivity step; an explicit computation would confirm the negation of falsifier F8.” Voisin’s examples [Voi02] show that the Hodge conjecture fails for compact Kähler manifolds that are not projective.

**Proposed by.** [S05].

**Background.** [Voi02]; [Voi02b].

**Solved when.** An explicit computation of the prism obstruction class for the tori of [Voi02], showing that it is the projectivity obstruction of [S05, Theorem 37.2.4]; a computation showing that it is a different class refutes the prediction.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No prism-theoretic analysis of Voisin’s examples was found outside [S05].

**Discussion.** [Issue 48](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/48). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
