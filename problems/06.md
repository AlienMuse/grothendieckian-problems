# Problem 6 · Verified encodings of universal properties

**Kind.** Formalization problem, research level. Thesis 3 ([A structured latent space](../THESES.md#thesis-3)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. III, §18.5, p. 8307, and §21.16, item 8, p. 8370. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** O (obligation).

**Statement.** The component MUF-H (Motivic Universal Factorization Algorithm—Hybrid) of [S01] solves universal-property constraints for finite proxies of categories. “A solver that returns a satisfying assignment only proves satisfiability of the encoded finite constraints if the encoding and certificate are independently checked. It does not establish that the encoding captures the intended universal property.” For finite categories given by their composition tables, give an encoding of the statement that a cone (or cocone) is a limit (colimit) of a finite diagram into constraints a solver can decide, and prove the encoding correct.

**What the source establishes.** The source requires that “Universality encodings still require an independently checked correspondence between mathematical quantifiers and constraints”, and that existence of mediating maps and their uniqueness be recorded as separate obligations, since “Universality requires a quantified mediating property and generally uniqueness at an explicitly stated identity strength.”

**Proposed by.** [S01].

**Background.** [Mac78]; [MM94].

**Solved when.** A machine-checked theorem that, for every finite diagram in a finite category, the solutions of the encoded constraints correspond bijectively to mediating morphisms and that the uniqueness check is complete, together with a verified checker for solver certificates.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** General work on constraint solving and finite model finding was found; no verified encoding of universal properties was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 6](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/6). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
