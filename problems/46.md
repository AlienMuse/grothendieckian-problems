# Problem 46 · Independence of the twelve prism axioms

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, entry level. Thesis 12 ([Judge a method by its open ledger](../THESES.md#thesis-12)).

**Source.** *The Archimedean Prism* [S05], Problem 71.8.1, p. 1026; dossier, p. 1032. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23597954](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23597954).

**Status in the source.** open.

**Statement.** “Prove that the twelve prism axioms AP1–AP12 are logically independent by exhibiting, for each, a model satisfying the other eleven and failing it.”

**What the source establishes.** “Theorem 46.4.1 states the axioms; their independence is asserted but not demonstrated.” The dossier: “The twelve axioms are individually motivated, but motivation does not prove logical independence.”

**Proposed by.** [S05].

**Background.** Hodge theory [Del71]; the axioms and their sources are in [S05, Ch. 46].

**Solved when.** Twelve models, the $k$-th satisfying every axiom except AP$k$, each verified against the definitions of [S05, Ch. 46]; or a proof that one axiom follows from the others, which is a correction to the source.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No work on these axioms outside [S05] was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 46](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/46). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
