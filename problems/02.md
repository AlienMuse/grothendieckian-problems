# Problem 2 · A bounded fixed point of the coupled world–language cycle

**Kind.** Formalization problem, programme level. Thesis 10 ([Discovery is recursive](../THESES.md#thesis-10)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. III, §75.17, pp. 9503–9504; §75.11, pp. 9499–9500; §56.15, pp. 9130–9131. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** O (obligation).

**Statement.** Give a formal transition model of the coupled world–language cycle of [S01, Vol. III, Ch. 71–78] and prove, in a proof assistant, three properties of it: (a) the obligation-preservation inclusion $O^{\mathrm{open}}_n \subseteq O^{\mathrm{open}}_{n+1} \cup O^{\mathrm{dis}}_{n+1} \cup O^{\mathrm{term}}_{n+1}$ of §75.11 holds for every accepted step; (b) the six conditions of §75.17 under which “A cycle reaches BOUNDED_FIXED_POINT” are decidable from the records; and (c) every run terminates, since “A conforming campaign therefore carries a well-founded repair measure or a hard policy bound.”

**What the source establishes.** The conditions are stated as contracts; none is proved. Failure of the inclusion in (a) “is sufficient for refusal or quarantine, regardless of the attractiveness of the new result.” The source limits what a fixed point means: “This is not equality of world and language, global convergence, consistency, optimality, or future stability.”

**Proposed by.** [S01].

**Background.** Transition systems and invariants in the style of [HW90]; mechanised metatheory [BW05].

**Solved when.** A machine-checked development (Lean, Rocq, Isabelle or Agda) that defines the model, documents its correspondence with §§75.11 and 75.17 clause by clause, and proves (a)–(c); or a machine-checked counterexample showing that one of them fails in every model faithful to the source.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No formal model of this cycle was found; the searches returned general work on invariants in refinement-based verification only.

**Discussion.** [Issue 2](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/2). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
