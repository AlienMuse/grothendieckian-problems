# Problem 51 · The real-place gauge

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 8 ([The rising sea](../THESES.md#thesis-8)).

**Source.** *Strained Prismatic Geometry* [S06], Problem 62.1.7 (rank 4 of the critical path), p. 1276. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23610034](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23610034).

**Status in the source.** OPEN.

**Statement.** In the analytic-stack and condensed setting of [S06, Ch. 17], “Construct the gauge stack of PO-017; prove the uniqueness, finite-place compatibility, coherent all-place gluing, Arakelov degree comparison, and exceptional-operation functoriality of PO-018–PO-022; and settle the archimedean coefficient overlap and dualisability issues represented by PO-097–PO-098.”

**What the source establishes.** The target is typed but no existence theorem is available. The source gives the falsifier: “A non-zero principal-rescaling defect, failure of real descent, or failure of dualisability for the required leg destroys this version of the all-place assembly.”

**Proposed by.** [S06].

**Background.** [Sch19]; [BS22]; [Efi24].

**Solved when.** A construction with proofs of PO-017–PO-022 and PO-097–PO-098, or an instance of one of the three named falsifiers.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No construction of an archimedean gauge stack of this kind was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 51](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/51). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
