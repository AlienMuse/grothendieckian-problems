# Problem 45 · No δ-structure on the archimedean prism

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 9 ([State what is not established](../THESES.md#thesis-9)).

**Source.** *The Archimedean Prism* [S05], Problem 71.3.4, p. 1023; dossier, p. 1029. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23597954](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23597954).

**Status in the source.** open.

**Statement.** “Make precise, as a no-go theorem, the claim that the archimedean prism carries no 𝛿-structure and hence no literal Frobenius lift.”

**What the source establishes.** “Chapter 16 argues the analogy with prismatic cohomology is structural only; a no-go theorem would settle the point rather than assert it.” The dossier sets the standard: “A no-go theorem must quantify over all functorial candidate operations and identify an invariant they cannot satisfy; observing that the current construction lacks one is not enough.”

**Proposed by.** [S05].

**Background.** δ-rings [Joy85; Bui05]; prisms [BS22].

**Solved when.** A theorem that quantifies over all functorial operations on the archimedean prism of [S05, Ch. 13–17] in a stated sense and proves that none is a δ-structure (or a Frobenius lift), by exhibiting an invariant that every such operation would have to satisfy and does not; or a functorial δ-structure, which would refute the claim.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No result on δ-structures for archimedean or real Hodge-theoretic analogues of prisms was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 45](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/45). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
