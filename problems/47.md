# Problem 47 · Descent beyond the polarized case

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 5 ([Report failure; abstain without evidence](../THESES.md#thesis-5)).

**Source.** *The Archimedean Prism* [S05], Problem 71.3.3, p. 1023; dossier, p. 1029. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23597954](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23597954).

**Status in the source.** open.

**Statement.** “Determine whether the descent theorem Theorem 15.4.2 holds for non-polarisable mixed Hodge structures, or exhibit the first obstruction to it.”

**What the source establishes.** In the source, “descent along the prism is proved only in the polarised pure case.” The dossier explains what changes: “Nonpolarisable mixed structures have nonsemisimple extension data and weight filtrations that need not split; automorphism groupoids can cease to be reductive”, and notes that the answer may be a counterexample rather than an extension theorem.

**Proposed by.** [S05].

**Background.** [Del71]; [Sch73]; [CDK95].

**Solved when.** A proof of the descent theorem for non-polarisable mixed Hodge structures in a stated class, or an explicit mixed Hodge structure for which descent fails, with the obstruction identified.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No result on descent along the archimedean prism was found outside [S05].

**Discussion.** [Issue 47](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/47). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
