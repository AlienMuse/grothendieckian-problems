# Problem 25 · The Beck–Chevalley square 12.6.3

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 4 ([Attention as descent](../THESES.md#thesis-4)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], §2.4.4, pp. 157–158 (Proposition 2.4.24, Theorem 2.4.25). DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** globally open.

**Statement.** For a context family $\mathrm{Rep}\colon\mathcal C_{\mathrm{ref}}\to\mathrm{Cat}_\infty$, transport along refinements is coherent by straightening, but “Straightening supplies no Beck–Chevalley comparison for that square” formed by a refinement arrow and a cover restriction, and Proposition 2.4.24 shows that “The gap is real even in discrete categories.” Characterise the families and covers for which the comparison $\rho_V\circ r_!\Rightarrow (r_i)_!\circ\rho_U$ exists, is invertible, and satisfies the identity, composition and overlap coherence laws, so that the conditional Theorem 2.4.25 applies.

**What the source establishes.** Theorem 2.4.25 proves coverwise transport under exactly these hypotheses, and Chapter 13.5 audits finite fixtures; the source calls this “a scope restriction and conditional local closure, not a general solution.” “The monograph does not close the gap globally.”

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** [Lur09]; [MM94]; [SGA4].

**Solved when.** A theorem giving necessary and sufficient conditions, in a stated generality, for the comparison of Theorem 2.4.25 to exist with its coherences, or a proof that no condition weaker than the hypothesis itself suffices in that generality.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Recent work on Beck–Chevalley fibrations concerns ambidexterity and norm squares; no result on refinement–cover squares of this kind was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 25](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/25). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
