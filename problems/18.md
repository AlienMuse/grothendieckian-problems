# Problem 18 · Discovering the site

**Kind.** Machine problem, programme level. Thesis 3 ([A structured latent space](../THESES.md#thesis-3)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], §1.2.5, p. 22. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** open (listed among “The five things we are least sure of”).

**Statement.** In the architecture of [S02] a context is a site, the information it supports is a sheaf, and a conclusion is a global section. The site itself is supplied by hand: “The site must be imposed. We do not know how to discover the evidence-atom locale from a corpus; we construct it.” Give a procedure that constructs the evidence-atom locale, its refinement order and its covering families from a corpus, with a stated sufficiency criterion, and show that the result does not merely reflect the design of the procedure.

**What the source establishes.** The source states the objection it cannot answer: “a critic who says the invariants measure our design choices rather than the model’s structure has a real point that we cannot fully answer.” Learned restriction maps are studied elsewhere on a fixed graph [BDCLB22; DBDL24]; the base space is not learned there.

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** [HG19]; [BDCLB22]; [DBDL24]; [MM94].

**Solved when.** A procedure and a published evaluation in which (i) planted sites in synthetic corpora are recovered up to a declared isomorphism budget, (ii) the site constructed from a natural corpus is stable under resampling within that budget, and (iii) invariants computed on it survive the gaming controls of [S02, §9.1] (terminal-site collapse, zero restrictions, filled nerve).

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Sheaf neural networks with learned restriction maps on a given graph were found [BDCLB22; DBDL24]; no procedure that learns the site itself was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 18](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/18). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
