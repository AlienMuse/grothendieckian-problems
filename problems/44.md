# Problem 44 · The Hodge decomposition in Lean

**Kind.** Formalization problem, programme level. Thesis 9 ([State what is not established](../THESES.md#thesis-9)).

**Source.** *The Archimedean Prism* [S05], Problem 71.11.1, p. 1027; dossier, p. 1034; The five volumes, p. xv. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23597954](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23597954).

**Status in the source.** open.

**Statement.** “Formalise the Hodge decomposition for compact Kähler manifolds in Lean 4 with mathlib, the single largest absent prerequisite identified in Chapter 64.”

**What the source establishes.** The source records the verdict that “the Hodge conjecture cannot presently even be stated in Lean 4 with mathlib”, and estimates the formalisation “as an effort on the order of the Liquid Tensor Experiment.” Its dossier limits what success means: “Success would enable formal statements of the later conjectures; it would prove none of them by itself.” Problem 71.11.2 (Chow groups and the cycle class map) is the other missing prerequisite.

**Proposed by.** [S05].

**Background.** [Voi02b]; [mC20]; [Sch22].

**Solved when.** A Lean 4 development, accepted into Mathlib or released as a library building against it, that defines compact Kähler manifolds and proves the Hodge decomposition of their de Rham cohomology.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** GitHub code search (2026-10-28) found in mathlib a Hodge star on exterior powers and algebraic Kähler differentials, but no Kähler manifolds and no Hodge decomposition; formal-conjectures contains no Hodge-theoretic statement.

**Discussion.** [Issue 44](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/44). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
