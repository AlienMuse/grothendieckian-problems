# Problem 7 · The AURORA-K0 kernel, I: syntax and checking

**Kind.** Formalization problem, research level. Thesis 2 ([Status is part of the type](../THESES.md#thesis-2)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. III, §70.19, pp. 9428–9429 (obligations K0-META-001 to K0-META-009). DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** O (“Every item begins OPEN”).

**Statement.** Mechanise AURORA-K0, the small formal kernel of the experimental calculus AURORA [S01, Vol. III, Ch. 70], and discharge the first nine obligations of its registry, among them that the “Concrete grammar has unique parsing after canonical Unicode normalization”, that “Substitution preserves K0 typing”, that “Registered reduction preserves typing”, that “Algorithmic checking agrees with declarative K0 rules”, and that the “Definitional equality procedure is decidable for the declared finite fragment”.

**What the source establishes.** The registry lists each obligation with the evidence required before its status may change, and rules out the shortcut: “No row may become RECEIPT_BOUND merely because a checker returns success.” No obligation is discharged.

**Proposed by.** [S01].

**Background.** The de Bruijn criterion and small kernels [BW05]; [MU21]; [Car24].

**Solved when.** A development in a proof assistant that defines K0 as in the exact edition of Chapter 70 and proves K0-META-001 to K0-META-009 using no axioms beyond the assistant’s standard ones; a machine-checked counterexample to any item also counts and is recorded as a correction to the source.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Mechanised metatheory of type theories is an established field; no mechanisation of K0 was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 7](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/7). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
