# Problem 13 · A frozen benchmark for counterexample and proof discovery

**Kind.** Machine problem, entry level. Thesis 12 ([Judge a method by its open ledger](../THESES.md#thesis-12)).

**Source.** *GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine* [S01], Vol. IV, §113.1, p. 11744; §113.12.12 to §113.12.15, pp. 11763–11764. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23480696](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480696).

**Status in the source.** D (specified, not run).

**Statement.** Release the first version of the benchmark specified in [S01, Vol. IV, Ch. 113]. “A benchmark is not merely a directory of examples and a script that prints a score.” Before any run, it “must freeze the task, version, population, split, metric, abstention policy, leakage boundary, resource model, and reporting semantics”, and it must separate counterexample tasks into strata A–F and proof tasks into the seven types of §113.12.13.

**What the source establishes.** The specification is complete and unexecuted. Its scoring rules include that “Stratum E evaluates whether the system reports bounded unknown honestly; it does not reward a claim that the theorem is true”, and that “Hidden proofs are protected evaluation assets, not canonical solution paths.”

**Proposed by.** [S01].

**Background.** [ZHP22]; [FLM+26].

**Solved when.** A release with the four boundaries of §113.1 frozen by digest, at least one item in every counterexample stratum and every proof-task type, a working challenge process (§113.12.15), and results for at least two systems reported stratum by stratum.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Benchmarks for formal and research mathematics exist [ZHP22; FLM+26]; none found has the stratification, the abstention strata or the frozen constitution of Ch. 113.

**Discussion.** [Issue 13](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/13). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
