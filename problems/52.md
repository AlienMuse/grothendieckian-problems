# Problem 52 · The gluing problem for the global substrate

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, programme level. Thesis 8 ([The rising sea](../THESES.md#thesis-8)).

**Source.** *Strained Prismatic Geometry* [S06], Problem 18.1.5, p. 352. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23610034](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23610034).

**Status in the source.** open.

**Statement.** Construct an analytic stack $\mathfrak S$ with morphisms $\mathfrak S_v\to\mathfrak S$ for every place $v$ of $\mathbb Q$ and a morphism $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z\to\mathfrak S$ such that (i) $D(\mathfrak S)$ maps to $D(\mathfrak S_v)$ for every $v$, compatibly with the rational realisations; (ii) the local structures — Frobenius and Nygaard filtration at finite $v$, weight filtration and dilation at $\infty$ — are matched by the comparison data; and (iii) $\mathfrak S$ is not terminal.

**What the source establishes.** “Condition (iii) is what makes the problem non-trivial: a terminal object satisfies (i) and (ii) vacuously.” The legs can be compared only through $\mathbb Q$ (Remark 18.1.4), and Obstruction 18.1.6 shows that a Frobenius datum on a glued object degenerates at infinity.

**Proposed by.** [S06].

**Background.** [Sch19]; [BL22]; [Dri20].

**Solved when.** A construction satisfying (i)–(iii), or a proof that every analytic stack satisfying (i) and (ii) is terminal.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No construction of this kind was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 52](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/52). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
