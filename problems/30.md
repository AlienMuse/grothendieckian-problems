# Problem 30 · Monotone refinement budgets

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 5 ([Report failure; abstain without evidence](../THESES.md#thesis-5)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], Question 11.11.19, p. 2259. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** X.

**Statement.** The recomputed refinement budget of [S02, Proposition 11.9.20] is not monotone along refinement. Question 11.11.19: “Is there a natural class of covers — closed under the refinements one actually performs — on which some useful instrument is monotone?” The source names two candidate classes, nerve-preserving refinements and split-only refinements.

**What the source establishes.** For both candidates the needed facts are unproved, and “No such theorem is currently available.” A proved monotone direction would let the diameter of a totally ordered class be read at its endpoints.

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** Cellular sheaves and their cohomology [HG19].

**Solved when.** A theorem identifying such a class and an instrument monotone on it, or a proof that each of the two named classes admits no useful monotone instrument.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** No result on monotone instruments under cover refinement was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 30](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/30). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
