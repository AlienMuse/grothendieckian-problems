# Problem 54 · The first falsification test for GL$_2$

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 9 ([State what is not established](../THESES.md#thesis-9)).

**Source.** *The Arithmetic Topos* [S07], Problem 28.28, pp. 1108–1109. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23629681](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23629681).

**Status in the source.** CONJECTURE (a falsification test).

**Statement.** “For the standard GL2 monoid over Q, construct a global distribution on the rank-one stratum whose local normal residue is the coefficient of” Proposition 28.16 of [S07], “and prove that its spectral expansion is exactly the sum of the trivial, special, and maximal-parabolic terms removed in Altuğ’s Poisson reorganisation.”

**What the source establishes.** A mismatch of coefficients would refute the simplest form of the source’s Conjecture 28.20. The problem precedes functoriality on purpose: “It asks whether the diagnosis reproduces a known rank-one calculation without changing its constants. A framework that cannot pass this comparison has no claim on the general problem.”

**Proposed by.** [S07]; the rank-one calculation is Altuğ’s [Alt15].

**Background.** [Alt15]; [EEKW24].

**Solved when.** The distribution constructed and its spectral expansion proved equal to the terms removed in [Alt15], or a proof that the coefficients differ.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Altuğ’s Beyond Endoscopy computations for GL$_2$ over the rationals [Alt15] and their extension to totally real fields, which removes the trivial and special representations by Poisson summation on the regular elliptic terms [EEKW24], were found; no boundary-resolution reading of them was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 54](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/54). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
