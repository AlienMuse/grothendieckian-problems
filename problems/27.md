# Problem 27 · Local dynamics of the restriction-frame locus

**Kind.** Mathematical problem, research level. Thesis 4 ([Attention as descent](../THESES.md#thesis-4)).

**Source.** *GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence* [S02], Table 13.16.13, entry O8, p. 3174. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23538544](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538544).

**Status in the source.** OPEN.

**Statement.** Determine “whether the head-indexed restriction-frame embedded locus is attracting, repelling or a saddle under unfrozen frame dynamics”, the open entry O8 of the unproved register of [S02, Part P].

**What the source establishes.** Proposition 13.11.46 proves a parameter-specific departure from the locus and refutes universal invariance, but does not classify the local dynamics. The register names the missing tool: “a Lyapunov, centre-manifold or other local-dynamics argument for the frame manifold”.

**Proposed by.** [S02].

**Background.** [Car81]; optimisation on matrix manifolds [AMS08].

**Solved when.** A proof classifying the locus as attracting, repelling or a saddle (possibly depending on stated parameters) under the dynamics of Chapter 13.11.

**Literature check (28 October 2026).** Work on Stiefel-manifold training dynamics was found; no classification of this locus was found.

**Discussion.** [Issue 27](https://github.com/AlienMuse/grothendieckian-problems/issues/27). Solutions, partial results, counterexamples to the problem as stated and reports that it is already solved are all welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
